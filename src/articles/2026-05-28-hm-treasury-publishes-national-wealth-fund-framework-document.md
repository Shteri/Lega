## Background

The National Wealth Fund was established as part of the UK Government's industrial strategy and clean energy transition agenda. It channels public capital into strategic investment priorities.

The NWF is a government-owned company. The Framework Document is the constitutional instrument governing HM Treasury's oversight of it. The NWF sits within the broader landscape of public financial institutions, alongside the British Business Bank and the UK Infrastructure Bank, which has now been absorbed into the NWF.

## Developments

On 28 May 2026, HM Treasury published the National Wealth Fund Framework Document. The document was published jointly by HM Treasury and the National Wealth Fund.

The document sets out the governance, accountability and operational relationships between three parties: the NWF, its Shareholder (HM Treasury) and the Shareholder Representative (UK Government Investments). It defines the NWF's core responsibilities and the parameters within which the NWF is expected to operate.

The document describes the day-to-day relationship between the three parties and the formal governance structures that apply. It establishes how public accountability is maintained over the NWF's investment activities.

The full Framework Document is a 53-page PDF with a file size of 414 KB. It is available from GOV.UK.
