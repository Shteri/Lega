1. HM Treasury and HMRC have published version 1.32 of the Tariff of the United Kingdom, dated 25 May 2026, which enters into force on 1 July 2026.
2. Version 1.32 replaces version 1.30 (dated 14 January 2026, in force from 22 January 2026), which has been deleted.
3. Version 1.31 (dated 3 March 2026, in force from 1 April 2026) remains concurrently available.
4. The reference document sets out the UK's most favoured nation (MFN) tariff rates, known as the UK Global Tariff, and establishes the commodity code structure used to classify goods imported into the UK.
5. The updates are made under section 8 of the Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018 and referenced in The Customs Tariff (Establishment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.
6. The document was originally published on 16 December 2020 and has been updated continuously, with version history showing at least 32 versions since inception.

## Background

The reference document is part of the UK trade framework established after the UK left the European Union. Under this framework, the UK set up its own independent customs tariff and commodity code structure, replacing the EU Common External Tariff.

The document sets the UK's most favoured nation tariff rates, known as the UK Global Tariff. It also establishes the commodity code structure used to classify goods imported into the UK. It is referenced in The Customs Tariff (Establishment) (EU Exit) Regulations 2020.

The tariff is administered by HMRC and HM Treasury under the Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018. Under section 8(5) of that Act, HM Treasury must have regard to the interests of consumers and producers in the UK, the desirability of maintaining and promoting external trade and productivity, and the extent to which the goods are subject to competition when setting tariff rates. Under section 8(6), HM Treasury must also have regard to the recommendation of the Secretary of State.

## Developments

HM Treasury and HMRC have published version 1.32 of the Tariff of the United Kingdom. It is dated 25 May 2026 and enters into force on 1 July 2026. The file size of version 1.32 is 4.12 MB.

Version 1.32 replaces version 1.30, which was dated 14 January 2026 and in force from 22 January 2026. Version 1.30 has been deleted.

Version 1.31, dated 3 March 2026 and in force from 1 April 2026, remains concurrently available. The file size of version 1.31 is 4.1 MB.

The document affects UK importers of goods, customs agents and freight forwarders, UK producers and manufacturers competing with imports, and traders relying on UK commodity code classification. HMRC administers the tariff, and HM Treasury sets the tariff rates.

## What to Watch

Version 1.32 enters into force on 1 July 2026. Until that date, version 1.31 (in force from 1 April 2026) remains available alongside it. The reference document has been updated continuously since its original publication on 16 December 2020.
