## Key Takeaways

1. HM Treasury published the April 2026 Preston factors, made up of earnings and interest factors, on 1 June 2026.
2. The factors are for employers administering retrospective access to occupational pension schemes for part-time workers.
3. They follow the European Court of Justice and House of Lords rulings in 2000/2001 in favour of part-timers gaining retrospective access to occupational pension schemes, subject to meeting necessary legal requirements.
4. The factors are used to provide those entitled to reinstatement an opportunity to gain pension service at an actuarially fair cost.
5. The stated aim is to, 'as far as possible make these individuals no better and no worse off than if they had paid contributions to the scheme when they were originally employed.'
6. Two Excel spreadsheets are provided: the Preston guidance spreadsheet (94.7 KB) and a separate Employee NICs Rebate spreadsheet for April 2026 (55.7 KB).
7. The guidance is categorised under 'Government efficiency, transparency and accountability' and forms part of the HMT Preston guidance collection.

## Background

The Preston factors arise from court rulings in 2000 and 2001. The European Court of Justice ruled in 2000, and the House of Lords ruled in 2001, in favour of part-time workers gaining retrospective access to occupational pension schemes, subject to meeting necessary legal requirements. These rulings stem from the Preston v Wolverhampton Healthcare NHS Trust case.

This guidance concerns occupational pensions and employment law obligations arising from the Preston litigation. It sits outside the core financial services regulatory perimeter. It is an administrative and actuarial update by HM Treasury to assist employers in complying with the court-mandated obligations to part-time workers.

The factors are used to calculate the actuarially fair cost of reinstating pension service for those entitled. The aim is to leave individuals neither better nor worse off than if they had paid contributions at the time of their original employment.

## Developments

HM Treasury published the April 2026 Preston factors on 1 June 2026. The factors consist of earnings factors and interest factors and apply from 1 April 2026. They are for use by employers administering retrospective occupational pension scheme access for qualifying part-time workers.

Two Excel spreadsheets are provided. One contains the Preston factors and has a file size of 94.7 KB. The other contains the Employee NICs Rebate calculations for April 2026 and has a file size of 55.7 KB.

The guidance is relevant to employers administering occupational pension schemes, part-time workers entitled to retrospective access, pension scheme administrators, and HR and payroll professionals handling NICs rebate calculations.
