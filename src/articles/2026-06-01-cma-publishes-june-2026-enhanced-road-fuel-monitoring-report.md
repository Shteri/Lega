## Background

The report forms part of the CMA's ongoing enhanced road fuel monitoring programme. This programme was established following the CMA's 2023 market study into road fuel pricing.

The monitoring work is underpinned by data-gathering powers conferred by the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024. It sits within the CMA's broader consumer-facing market oversight work, rather than its digital platforms or SMS designation functions.

## Developments

On 1 June 2026, the CMA published its June 2026 enhanced road fuel monitoring report. The report is 36 pages long and the file size is 2.15 MB.

The report assesses the impact of the conflict in the Middle East on UK road fuel prices, retail spreads and margins during March and April 2026. It analyses differences in petrol and diesel prices between different types of retail outlets, and examines the drivers of price and margin trends.

The report covers fuel margins for supermarket and other fuel retailers from January 2025 to April 2026. It includes average UK pump prices and their components from January 2022 to May 2026. It also includes average UK pump prices and aggregate inventories for the four largest supermarkets from December 2025 to May 2026.

The CMA gathered the underlying data using its information-gathering powers under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024. The entities covered by the report include UK road fuel retailers, the four largest supermarket fuel retailers, non-supermarket fuel retailers, and UK consumers purchasing petrol and diesel.

## What to Watch

The report sets out next steps in the CMA's approach to monitoring the road fuel market.
