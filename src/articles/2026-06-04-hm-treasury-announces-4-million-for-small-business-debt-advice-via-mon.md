## Background

The announcement sits within the government's Financial Inclusion Strategy, which was launched in November 2025. The strategy aims to remove barriers to financial participation and build household financial resilience.

It also forms part of the Plan for Small Business. This plan includes reforms to late payments and access to finance.

The funding reflects ongoing HM Treasury and Money and Pensions Service efforts to extend debt advice to underserved groups. This includes sole traders and the self-employed, who fall at the intersection of personal and business finance.

## Developments

HM Treasury has announced £4 million in funding over three years to expand business debt advice services through the Money and Pensions Service. The funding builds on the existing Business Debtline service delivered by the Money Advice Trust.

The funding is intended to support an additional 16,000 small businesses and sole traders, beyond the 50,000 already supported. This brings the total to 75,000 businesses over the next three years.

The new money builds on the £3 million per year that the Money and Pensions Service has provided since 2024 for the Business Debtline service. That service has supported around 50,000 people, with more than 90% of clients seeing their debts reduce or stabilise.

A further £2 million of separate funding will go towards modernising debt advice services. This is added to the £4.8 million already invested under the Financial Inclusion Strategy.

Economic Secretary to the Treasury Rachel Blake MP made the announcement on 4 June 2026. Money and Pensions Service CEO Oliver Morley and Money Advice Trust CEO Steve Vaid also provided statements.

Previous recipients of the modernisation fund have delivered projects including AI pilots, digital referral pathways, upgraded infrastructure, and more inclusive service design. Money Advice Plus reduced first appointment times by 46% following a £49,000 technology investment, which included AI-supported transcription. After modernisation, 97% of Money Advice Plus staff reported reliable systems. Citizens Advice SORT achieved 100% systematic case-checking capacity through AI-driven verification and 80% time savings in semi-automated case write-ups.

## What to Watch

The £4 million funding boost is set to run over three years, with the aim of supporting an additional 16,000 small businesses and sole traders during that period. The £2 million modernisation fund will continue work begun under the Financial Inclusion Strategy launched in November 2025.
