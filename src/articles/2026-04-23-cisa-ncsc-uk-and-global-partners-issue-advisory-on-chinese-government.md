## Key Takeaways

1. On 23 April 2026, CISA, the UK's National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) and international partners jointly issued an advisory about covert cyber networks linked to the Chinese government.
2. The advisory is directed at critical infrastructure operators and other entities at risk from state-sponsored threat actors that use concealed network infrastructure.
3. CISA published the advisory as a press release on the same date that it issued a separate warning about FIRESTARTER malware targeting Cisco ASA, Firepower and Secure Firewall products.
4. The publication reflects continued coordination among Five Eyes and allied partners on attribution of Chinese cyber activity and on defensive guidance.
5. The full text of the advisory is not reproduced in the source document; only the title and date of the press release are confirmed from the CISA news listing page.

## Background

The advisory is part of a sustained Five Eyes and allied effort to publicly attribute and disrupt Chinese state-sponsored cyber operations targeting critical infrastructure. It follows prior joint advisories on Volt Typhoon and other Chinese APT activity.

It sits within a broader regulatory effort to mandate incident reporting and harden critical infrastructure against nation-state threats. Relevant measures include the Cyber Incident Reporting for Critical Infrastructure Act (CIRCIA) in the United States, the UK Network and Information Systems (NIS) Regulations, and the forthcoming UK Cyber Security and Resilience Bill. CISA's threat advisory functions are authorised under the National Defense Authorization Act.

## Developments

On 23 April 2026, CISA, the UK NCSC and global partners jointly issued an advisory concerning covert cyber networks linked to the Chinese government. The advisory targets critical infrastructure operators, network and information systems operators, government agencies, and private sector entities at risk from Chinese state-sponsored threat actors that use concealed network infrastructure.

CISA published the advisory as a press release. On the same date, CISA also issued a separate warning about FIRESTARTER malware targeting Cisco ASA, Firepower and Secure Firewall products. Organisations using Cisco ASA and related firewall products are affected by that co-incident advisory.

The source document confirms only the title and date of the press release from the CISA news listing page. The full text of the joint advisory is not reproduced in that document.

## What to Watch

The UK Cyber Security and Resilience Bill is forthcoming. Affected entities can monitor CISA and NCSC channels for the full advisory text, including any indicators and mitigations referenced in the press release.
