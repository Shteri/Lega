## Background

Treasury Minutes are a standard parliamentary accountability mechanism. Through them, HM Treasury formally responds to recommendations made by the Committee of Public Accounts on behalf of the government.

The Treasury Minutes Progress Report is published as a regular series. It consolidates the government's outstanding and completed commitments arising from PAC scrutiny across public spending and policy. This includes areas relevant to financial services.

The June 2026 edition forms part of the government's efficiency, transparency and accountability framework. It relates to parliamentary accountability processes under the Committee of Public Accounts remit. No specific primary legislation is cited in the extracted text.

## Developments

HM Treasury published the Treasury Minutes Progress Report on 4 June 2026. The report records the government's progress in implementing PAC recommendations.

It covers government responses to recommendations spanning the following sessions: 2010-12, 2012-13, 2013-14, 2014-15, 2015-16, 2016-17, 2017-19, 2019, 2019-21, 2021-22, 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25, and 2025-26.

The report appears in two formats. The web version runs to 319 pages and is 5.2 MB. The print version runs to 322 pages and is 5.45 MB. The document is classified as a policy paper and forms part of the 'Treasury minutes progress report' collection.

The report affects HM Treasury, the Committee of Public Accounts, and all government departments subject to PAC recommendations. It also affects financial services regulators subject to PAC scrutiny, including the FCA, the PRA, the PSR and the Bank of England.
