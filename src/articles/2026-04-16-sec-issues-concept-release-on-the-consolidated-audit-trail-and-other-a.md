## Key Takeaways
1. On 16 April 2026, the SEC's Division of Trading and Markets issued Concept Release S7-2026-12 (Release No. 34-105251) on the Consolidated Audit Trail (CAT) and other audit trail and data sources.
2. The release is a non-binding solicitation of public views, not a proposed or final rule.
3. The release asks whether CAT remains the appropriate framework for regulatory data collection, how it might be modified or replaced, and how alternative data sources compare on cost, utility, and data security.
4. The release concerns audit trail data used in equity and options market surveillance.
5. Affected parties include national securities exchanges, FINRA and other self-regulatory organisations, broker-dealers that report to CAT, CAT LLC, and institutional and retail market participants subject to CAT reporting obligations.
6. The release was published for public comment on 16 April 2026.

## Background
The Consolidated Audit Trail was mandated under SEC Rule 613 of Regulation NMS, adopted in 2012. The rule was intended to create a comprehensive audit trail of all U.S. equity and options market activity.

The system has drawn criticism over its multi-billion dollar cost, which is borne by broker-dealers. It has also faced concerns over data security vulnerabilities involving personally identifiable information (PII), and questions about whether its surveillance utility justifies its expense compared with legacy audit trail systems such as OATS and EBS.

## Developments
The SEC issued Concept Release S7-2026-12 (Release No. 34-105251) on 16 April 2026. The release was filed under the Division of Trading and Markets.

The release is designated as a Concept Release. This means it is a non-rulemaking solicitation of public views rather than a proposed or final rule.

The release seeks input on three areas: whether CAT remains the appropriate framework for regulatory data collection, how it might be modified or replaced, and how alternative data sources compare in terms of cost, utility, and data security. It addresses the CAT alongside other audit trail and data sources used in equity and options market surveillance.

The rulemaking activity index confirms the release was published on 16 April 2026, with file number S7-2026-12 and release number 34-105251.

## What to Watch
The Concept Release was published for public comment on 16 April 2026. As a solicitation of views, it indicates the Commission is reconsidering the architecture and continued operation of the CAT system. Affected parties may submit comments in response to the questions posed in the release.
