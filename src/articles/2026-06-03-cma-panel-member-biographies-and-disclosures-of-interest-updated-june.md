## Background

The CMA panel is the independent decision-making body for Phase 2 merger and market investigations under the Enterprise Act 2002. It operates separately from the CMA Board to maintain independence.

Panel decisions are taken by independent groups of at least three panel members. Each group is led by an inquiry chair. The CMA maintains a public register of panel member biographies and disclosures of interest, and updates it regularly to meet its transparency obligations on conflicts of interest. The page was first published on 19 January 2016.

The panel's composition and members' interests carry added weight given the CMA's expanding remit under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024. That includes the new Strategic Market Status regime and enhanced consumer protection enforcement powers. The CMA's work also draws on the Competition Act 1998.

## Developments

The register was last updated on 3 June 2026. That update records a change to Kirstin Baker's disclosure of interests. Her disclosure was also updated on 30 March 2026.

The page captures a series of recent changes to panel composition. Richard Feasey was removed as Inquiry Chair on 30 April 2026. Humphrey Battcock and Mark Thatcher were removed as panel members on 2 April 2026. Cyrus Mehta was appointed as Inquiry Chair on 1 October 2025.

Several panel members were removed at the end of their terms. John Thanassoulis, Claire Whyley, Sheila McClelland and Jeremy Newman were removed on 6 October 2025. Ulrike Hotopp was removed on 9 December 2025. David Thomas was removed on 12 March 2026.

The register also logs updates to individual biographies and disclosures of interest. These include updates for Crispin Wright on 18 May 2026 and 13 October 2025, Martin Coleman on 28 April 2026, Dr Robin Cohen on 30 March 2026, Humphrey Battcock and Karthik Subramanya on 2 March 2026, Colleen Keck on 13 October 2025, Frances McLeman on 6 October 2025, and Maria Da Cunha on 7 August 2025. Broader updates to panel members' biographies and disclosed interests were recorded on 5 February 2026, 10 September 2025 and 16 January 2025.

As of 3 June 2026, the current inquiry chairs are Martin Coleman (Panel Chair), Kirstin Baker, Margot Daly and Cyrus Mehta.

These changes affect current and departing CMA panel members, the CMA inquiry chairs, parties to Phase 2 CMA mergers and markets investigations, and members of the public and businesses subject to CMA scrutiny.
