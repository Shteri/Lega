## Key Takeaways
1. HM Treasury has published a collection of Accounting Officer Assessment summaries covering Ministry of Justice (MOJ) programmes in the Government Major Projects Portfolio (GMPP).
2. The collection was first published on 25 July 2019 and was last updated on 27 May 2026, reflecting a rolling series of publications.
3. The documents cover prison expansion, probation reform, electronic monitoring, IT services, and secure schools.
4. The assessments record Accounting Officers' judgements on the regularity, propriety, value for money, and feasibility of major spending decisions.
5. The documents are published jointly by the Ministry of Justice and HM Treasury as transparency data.

## Background
Accounting Officer Assessments are transparency documents. In them, an Accounting Officer records their judgement on the regularity, propriety, value for money, and feasibility of a major government spending decision. These assessments are produced at key business case approval stages for projects in the Government Major Projects Portfolio (GMPP).

The GMPP framework covers large-scale government programmes. For the Ministry of Justice, this includes capital and operational programmes across criminal justice infrastructure and services. The collection supports public accountability for this spending, including scrutiny by Parliament and public accountability bodies.

## Developments
HM Treasury has published a collection of Accounting Officer Assessment summaries for Ministry of Justice programmes and projects in the GMPP. The page was first published on 25 July 2019 and was last updated on 27 May 2026.

The documents listed cover programmes including the Four New Prisons Project, the Probation Reform Programme, the Electronic Monitoring Expansion and Future Service Programme, the Oasis Restore (Medway) Secure School, the Community Accommodation Services Tier 3 (CAS3) National Roll-Out, the Prison Technology Transformation Programme, and multiple houseblock expansion and refurbishment projects. One Programme Business Case references a 2,500 houseblock expansion programme.

The assessments are published jointly by the Ministry of Justice and HM Treasury as transparency data under the GMPP framework. Affected entities include the Ministry of Justice, HM Treasury, and HM Prison and Probation Service.

## What to Watch
The collection is a rolling publication. The page was last updated on 27 May 2026, and further Accounting Officer Assessment summaries may be added as MOJ projects reach key business case approval stages.
