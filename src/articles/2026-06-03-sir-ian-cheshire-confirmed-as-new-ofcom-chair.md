## Key Takeaways
1. The Technology Secretary has confirmed Sir Ian Cheshire as the new Chair of Ofcom, the UK's independent communications regulator, as announced by the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT) on 3 June 2026.
2. The confirmation followed a pre-appointment hearing with the Science, Innovation and Technology Select Committee.
3. Sir Ian succeeds Lord Michael Grade, whose term as Ofcom Chair concluded at the end of April 2026.
4. Sir Ian was most recently Chair of Channel 4 from 2022 to 2025 and has previously held senior roles including Chief Executive of Kingfisher plc, Chair of Barclays UK, and Chair of Landsecurities PLC.
5. Sir Ian has committed to working constructively with Government on effective implementation of the Online Safety Act, delivering Ofcom's growth goals, and developing robust KPIs, while fully upholding Ofcom's operational independence.
6. Technology Secretary Liz Kendall stated that 'The Online Safety Act must be enforced robustly and without compromise, and Ofcom has a central role in making the UK the safest place to be online.'

## Background
Ofcom is the UK's independent communications regulator, established under the Communications Act 2003. It oversees the telecoms and broadcasting sectors.

The regulator's remit has expanded under the Online Safety Act 2023. Ofcom is implementing codes of practice and exercising its duties of care powers over user-to-user and search services. The Government has signalled intent to build further on the Online Safety Act.

## Developments
DSIT announced on 3 June 2026 that the Technology Secretary had confirmed Sir Ian Cheshire as the new Chair of Ofcom. The confirmation followed a pre-appointment hearing with the Science, Innovation and Technology Select Committee.

Sir Ian succeeds Lord Michael Grade, whose term as Ofcom Chair concluded at the end of April 2026. Sir Ian was most recently Chair of Channel 4 from 2022 to 2025. He has previously held senior roles including Chief Executive of Kingfisher plc, Chair of Barclays UK, and Chair of Landsecurities PLC.

Sir Ian has committed to working constructively with Government on effective implementation of the Online Safety Act, delivering Ofcom's growth goals, and developing robust KPIs. He has committed to fully upholding Ofcom's operational independence.

The appointment affects Ofcom, the UK telecoms and broadcasting sectors, and UK online platforms and services subject to Online Safety Act regulation.

## What to Watch
Sir Ian takes over as Ofcom continues the enforcement phase of the Online Safety Act 2023, including implementing codes of practice and exercising its duties of care powers over user-to-user and search services. The Government has signalled intent to build further on the Online Safety Act.
