## Key Takeaways

1. On 14 May 2026, the Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) opened an initial Strategic Market Status (SMS) investigation under Part 1 of the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 to assess whether Microsoft should be designated as having SMS in respect of its business software ecosystem.
2. The investigation will examine Microsoft UK customers' business software purchasing decisions, the alternatives available to them, and their ability to switch to those alternatives.
3. The CMA published an invitation to comment on 14 May 2026 setting out the scope of the investigation, the main avenues of investigation, and the potential issues and interventions being considered; it closed on 4 June 2026.
4. The CMA appointed Jigsaw Research to carry out qualitative customer research focused on Microsoft UK customers' purchasing decisions, available alternatives, and their ability to switch.
5. The indicative timetable provides for publication of a proposed SMS designation decision in October 2026, with a final decision report targeted for February 2027.
6. The statutory deadline for issuing any SMS decision notice is 13 February 2027.

## Background

The Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 (DMCCA 2024) came into force in January 2025. Part 1 of the Act created the SMS designation powers and placed the Digital Markets Unit on a statutory footing.

This investigation is one of the CMA's early exercises of its new SMS designation powers. It sits alongside other CMA digital markets work, including investigations into cloud services, mobile ecosystems, and AI foundation models. It is the first SMS investigation specifically targeting Microsoft's productivity and business software stack.

## Developments

The CMA formally launched the initial SMS investigation on 14 May 2026. On the same date, it sent an investigation notice to Microsoft and published an invitation to comment. The invitation to comment set out the scope of the investigation, the main avenues of investigation, and the potential issues and interventions being considered. The invitation to comment closed on 4 June 2026.

The investigation is examining whether Microsoft should be designated as having strategic market status in respect of its business software ecosystem. It will look at Microsoft UK customers' purchasing decisions for business software, the alternatives available to them, and their ability to switch to those alternatives.

On 5 June 2026, the CMA announced that it had appointed Jigsaw Research to undertake qualitative customer research. That research focuses on understanding Microsoft UK customers' business software purchasing decisions, the alternatives available to them, and their ability to switch.

The investigation is overseen by the Digital Markets Board Committee sub-committee. Its members are Sarah Cardell (CEO, CMA), Will Hayter (Executive Director for Digital Markets, CMA), Katie Kapernaros (Non-Executive Director, CMA), Colleen Keck (Panel Member, CMA), and Cyrus Mehta (Non-Executive Director, CMA).

The parties affected include Microsoft, Microsoft UK customers who purchase business software, competitors and alternative business software providers, third-party participants in the UK business software ecosystem, and the CMA Digital Markets Unit.

## What to Watch

Non-confidential responses to the invitation to comment are to be published by the end of June 2026. Initial evidence gathering and engagement with Microsoft and third parties is scheduled for May to July 2026.

Under the indicative timetable, the CMA expects to publish a proposed decision on SMS designation for consultation in October 2026. The consultation is expected to close in November 2026, when non-confidential responses would be published and Microsoft would have an opportunity to make oral representations.

The CMA expects to publish a final decision report and any SMS decision notice in February 2027. The statutory deadline for issuing any SMS decision notice is 13 February 2027.
