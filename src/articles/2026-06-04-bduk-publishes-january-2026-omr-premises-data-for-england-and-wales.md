1. Building Digital UK (BDUK) and the Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT) published premises-level transparency data on 4 June 2026, reflecting the January 2026 Open Market Review (OMR) determinations for England and Wales.
2. The data is provided at Unique Property Reference Number (UPRN) level and covers current connectivity, future commercial plans, and premises included in BDUK subsidy interventions.
3. The release is split into ten regional ZIP files covering nine English regions and Wales.

5. Separate publications exist for Scotland and Northern Ireland, so this release applies only to England and Wales.

## Background

Building Digital UK (BDUK) administers the UK government's broadband subsidy and infrastructure programmes. An Open Market Review (OMR) gathers information on where broadband networks already exist and where commercial providers plan to build, which helps BDUK identify areas that may need public subsidy.

As part of its transparency obligations around these programmes, BDUK publishes data showing its premises-level view of OMR determinations and subsidy interventions. This publication continues that practice and is distinct from the separate publications produced for Scotland and Northern Ireland.

The document does not directly cite primary legislation or statutory instruments.

## Developments

On 4 June 2026, BDUK and DSIT published premises-level data at UPRN level for England and Wales, using a reference date of January 2026 for the OMR determinations.

The release sets out BDUK's current view of premises-level data covering the January 2026 OMR determinations, current connectivity status, future commercial plans, and premises included in BDUK interventions.

The data is released as regional ZIP files covering ten areas: North East (16 MB), North West (44 MB), Yorkshire and the Humber (33.2 MB), East Midlands (29.1 MB), West Midlands (34.1 MB), East of England (37.2 MB), London (47.9 MB), South East (53.4 MB), South West (36.6 MB), and Wales (19.4 MB).

A UPRN level release user guide and a technical note accompany the data, along with a sample CSV file of 174 KB.

The entities affected include BDUK, DSIT, broadband network operators and commercial providers considering or operating in BDUK intervention areas, local authorities and devolved administrations in England and Wales, and premises owners and occupiers in areas subject to BDUK interventions or OMR determinations.

Separate publications cover Northern Ireland and Scotland, so this release applies only to England and Wales.
