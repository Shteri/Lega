1. HM Treasury published its monthly workforce management information for April 2026 on 26 May 2026.

3. HM Treasury states that these figures are not official statistics and have not been reconciled centrally with National Statistics.
4. The ONS quarterly public sector employment statistics remain the official headline measure for central government employment.

## Background

HM Treasury publishes workforce management information each month under the government's public sector workforce transparency framework. The figures are internal management information released in the interests of transparency.

These releases are not classified as official statistics. HM Treasury says the figures have not been reconciled centrally with National Statistics. The ONS quarterly public sector employment statistics are identified as the official headline measure for comparing the overall size of employment in central government organisations with other sectors of the economy at the relevant quarterly reference point.

## Developments

The April 2026 publication details monthly management information on staff numbers and paybill costs within HM Treasury and its agencies, with April 2026 as the reference month. It was published on 26 May 2026.

The document is provided as a Microsoft Excel spreadsheet of 55.2 KB and is available under the Open Government Licence v3.0. HM Treasury cautions that some organisations may not have information available for each month, and that at this stage coverage may therefore not reach 100% for those organisations in scope. The affected entities are HM Treasury and HM Treasury agencies.

## What to Watch

HM Treasury issues this workforce management information on a monthly basis, so further releases covering subsequent reference months can be expected.
