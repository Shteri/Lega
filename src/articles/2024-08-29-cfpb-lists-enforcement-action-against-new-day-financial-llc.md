## Key Takeaways

1. The CFPB's enforcement actions index page lists an action against New Day Financial, LLC, a mortgage lender, with a published and event date of 29 August 2024.
2. The document reviewed is the CFPB's enforcement actions index page and does not contain the full text of the New Day Financial order.
3. The specific allegations, violations, penalties, and relief terms for the New Day Financial action cannot be extracted from the index page.
4. The index page lists 386 filtered enforcement action results as of the document capture date.
5. The CFPB can enforce the law by filing an action in federal district court or by initiating an administrative adjudication proceeding conducted by an Administrative Law Judge.

## Background

New Day Financial, LLC is a non-depository mortgage lender. CFPB enforcement actions against non-bank mortgage lenders reflect the Bureau's focus on the mortgage origination sector following the Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act, which sets out the CFPB's enforcement authority.

The CFPB publishes an enforcement actions index page at consumerfinance.gov/enforcement/actions/. The page lists actions across multiple respondents and product categories, including mortgage origination, consumer reporting, debt collection, prepaid, and remittances. As of the document capture date, the page listed 386 filtered results, covering both administrative proceedings and civil actions.

## Developments

New Day Financial, LLC is listed in the index metadata as the subject of a CFPB enforcement action. The published and event date for that action is 29 August 2024. The index page confirms the action was filed on or around that date.

The raw text extracted from the index page does not include the body of the New Day Financial action. As a result, the allegations, violations, penalties, and relief terms cannot be determined from this document.

Other actions visible in the same index include actions against Synapse Financial Technologies (21 August 2025), Wise US Inc. (30 January 2025), Equifax (17 January 2025), Capital One (14 January 2025), Early Warning Services and Zelle banks (20 December 2024), and Navy Federal Credit Union (7 November 2024). The index describes Capital One as having over $480 billion in assets.

## What to Watch

The full details of the allegations and relief in the New Day Financial action would require the underlying consent order or complaint, which is not contained in the index page reviewed.
