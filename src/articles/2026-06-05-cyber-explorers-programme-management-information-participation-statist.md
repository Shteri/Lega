## Background

Cyber Explorers is described by the government as its cyber and digital skills programme for 11 to 14 year olds. It offers a free and interactive learning platform to help young people improve their digital skills and find out about careers in the cyber and digital sector.

The programme complements the school curriculum and can form part of in-school or after-school activities. According to DSIT, it supports the government's work to improve UK cyber defences, develop the cyber ecosystem and improve cyber security skills at all levels.

The programme sits within the UK government's cyber skills strategy, which addresses the long-term pipeline of cyber security talent. The published data is made available under the Open Government Licence v3.0. No primary legislation is cited in the document.

## Developments

DSIT has published its latest quarterly management information on the Cyber Explorers programme, covering the period to 31 March 2026.

The figures show cumulative engagement of 154,911 learners, 4,995 educators, and 3,549 schools by that date. The schools engaging with the programme include both state and independent schools.

The statistics are published quarterly and include breakdowns by number of pupils, teachers, schools, region, and location. Eleven quarterly data files have been published, spanning July 2023 to March 2026.

The dataset was first published on 23 January 2024. The most recent update, published on 5 June 2026, added Q4 FY2025-26 data covering January 2026 to March 2026.

## What to Watch

DSIT publishes these statistics quarterly. Further updates are expected to follow, adding engagement data for subsequent periods.
