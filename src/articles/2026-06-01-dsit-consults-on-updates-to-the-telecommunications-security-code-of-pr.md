## Key Takeaways
1. DSIT consulted on proposals to update the Telecommunications Security Code of Practice 2022, with the consultation open from 10am on 28 August 2025 to 11:59pm on 22 October 2025.
2. The proposed updates include drafting changes for greater clarity in Sections 1, 2 and 3 of the Code, additional measures in Section 3, and associated guidance in Section 2.
3. The updates are intended to help public telecoms providers protect UK telecoms networks and services in light of evolving threats and emerging technologies.
4. The consultation was open to anyone but particularly targeted providers of public electronic communications networks and services.
5. The proposed updates were set out in a 182-page PDF document of 2.94 MB.
6. The government published its formal response to the consultation on 1 June 2026, setting out how views raised were considered and where they resulted in revisions to the proposed updates.

## Background
The Telecommunications Security Code of Practice 2022 sets out security requirements for public telecoms providers. It sits within the UK's effort to implement and strengthen the telecommunications security framework established under the Telecommunications (Security) Act 2021, which underpins the Code.

The framework is part of the broader UK cyber resilience legislative landscape. This landscape also includes the forthcoming Cyber Security and Resilience Bill and the PSTI regime.

## Developments
The Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT) consulted on proposed updates to the Telecommunications Security Code of Practice 2022. The aim was to strengthen security requirements for public telecoms providers in light of evolving threats and emerging technologies.

The consultation opened at 10am on 28 August 2025 and closed at 11:59pm on 22 October 2025. It was open to anyone but was primarily targeted at providers of public electronic communications networks and providers of public electronic communications services.

The proposed updates were published in a document running to 182 pages (2.94 MB PDF). They included drafting clarifications in Sections 1, 2 and 3, additional security measures in Section 3, and associated guidance in Section 2.

On 1 June 2026, the government published its response to the consultation. The response explained how respondents' views were considered and where they resulted in revisions to the Code.
