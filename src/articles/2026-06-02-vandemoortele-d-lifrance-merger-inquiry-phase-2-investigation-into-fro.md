## Key Takeaways

1. The CMA is conducting a Phase 2 in-depth investigation into the completed acquisition by Vandemoortele Group of Délifrance S.A.
2. The investigation concerns the supply of frozen laminated dough products to retail and foodservice customers in the UK.
3. The CMA referred the merger for Phase 2 on 22 April 2026 after deciding not to accept the undertakings proposed by the parties.
4. On 15 May 2026, Vandemoortele Group notified the CMA that it accepts the merger may be expected to result in a substantial lessening of competition through horizontal unilateral effects.
5. The inquiry group accepted this concession and confirmed it will proceed with its investigation.
6. The statutory deadline for the Phase 2 investigation is 6 October 2026.

## Background

This is a UK merger control inquiry under Part 3 of the Enterprise Act 2002, conducted by the CMA's mergers function. It concerns the food manufacturing sector.

A "substantial lessening of competition", or SLC, is the legal test the CMA uses to decide whether a merger harms competition. A Phase 1 review is the CMA's initial assessment. If the CMA finds the merger may result in an SLC at Phase 1, it must refer the merger for a Phase 2 in-depth investigation, unless it accepts undertakings from the parties that address the competition concerns. Undertakings of this kind are known as undertakings in lieu of reference.

During an inquiry, the CMA can serve an initial enforcement order under section 72(2) of the Enterprise Act 2002. Such an order is used to prevent further integration of merging businesses while the investigation continues.

## Developments

The CMA opened its case on 3 October 2025 and launched its merger inquiry by notice to the parties on 10 October 2025. The inquiry concerns the completed acquisition by Vandemoortele Group of Délifrance S.A.

On 8 December 2025, the CMA announced its Phase 1 decision that the merger may be expected to result in a substantial lessening of competition within a market or markets in the United Kingdom. The merger was to be referred for Phase 2 unless undertakings were accepted.

On 22 December 2025, the CMA decided there were reasonable grounds to believe that undertakings offered by the parties might be accepted under the Enterprise Act 2002. On 24 December 2025, the CMA served an initial enforcement order under section 72(2) of the Enterprise Act 2002 on Safinco NV, Vandemoortele NV, Vamix NV, and Délifrance S.A.

On 18 February 2026, the CMA extended its consideration of the undertakings in lieu of reference until 22 April 2026. On 19 March 2026, the CMA published a consultation on the proposed undertakings in lieu of reference.

On 22 April 2026, the CMA referred the completed acquisition for a Phase 2 in-depth investigation and decided not to accept the proposed undertakings in lieu. On 23 April 2026, the CMA appointed the Phase 2 inquiry group, comprising Martin Coleman (Chair), Ashleye Gunn, and Sir Kenneth Parker.

On 15 May 2026, Vandemoortele Group notified the CMA that it accepts that the acquisition may be expected to result in a substantial lessening of competition as a result of horizontal unilateral effects in the supply of frozen laminated dough products to retail and foodservice customers in the UK. The inquiry group accepted Vandemoortele Group's SLC concession and confirmed it will proceed with its investigation accordingly.

## What to Watch

The statutory deadline for the Phase 2 investigation is 6 October 2026. The initial enforcement order served on Safinco NV, Vandemoortele NV, Vamix NV, and Délifrance S.A. under section 72(2) of the Enterprise Act 2002 remains relevant while the investigation continues.
