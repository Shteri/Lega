## Key Takeaways

1. The Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) has opened a merger inquiry into the anticipated acquisition by Co-operative Group Limited of Southern Co-operative Limited.
2. The case was opened on 4 June 2026, and both parties operate in the retail and wholesale sector.
3. The CMA has issued an invitation to comment that runs from 4 June 2026 to 18 June 2026.
4. Any interested party may submit initial views on the impact the transaction could have on competition in the UK.
5. Written representations must be sent to coopgroup.southerncoop@cma.gov.uk by 18 June 2026.
6. The CMA is processing the transaction under its merger control powers in Part 3 of the Enterprise Act 2002.

## Background

The CMA reviews mergers under the Enterprise Act 2002. Part 3 of that Act requires the CMA to assess whether a completed or anticipated merger may result in a substantial lessening of competition in the UK.

The CMA follows a set process. This begins with a pre-notification stage and an invitation to comment, before any formal Phase 1 investigation.

This case involves two co-operative retailers: Co-operative Group Limited and Southern Co-operative Limited. There are no digital markets or Online Safety Act dimensions to the case at this stage.

## Developments

The CMA opened a merger inquiry on 4 June 2026 into the anticipated acquisition by Co-operative Group Limited of Southern Co-operative Limited.

As of 4 June 2026, the CMA is in the pre-notification stage. It has received the information it needs from the parties to begin pre-notification, but it has not yet formally launched its Phase 1 investigation.

The CMA has issued an invitation to comment. This is the first step in the CMA's information-gathering process. The invitation runs from 4 June 2026 to 18 June 2026. It invites any interested party to submit initial views on the impact the transaction could have on competition in the UK.

Written representations about any competition issues must be submitted to coopgroup.southerncoop@cma.gov.uk by 18 June 2026. The contact telephone number for the case is 020 3738 6784.

The parties affected include Co-operative Group Limited, Southern Co-operative Limited, competitors in the retail and wholesale sector, and consumers in areas where both co-operatives operate.

## What to Watch

The deadline for interested parties to submit written representations in response to the invitation to comment is 18 June 2026. The formal Phase 1 investigation has not yet commenced.
