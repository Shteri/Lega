## Key Takeaways
1. The Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) published its 12th Annual Report on Concurrency on 2 June 2026.
2. The report covers the period from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026.
3. In the regulated sectors, both the CMA and the relevant sector regulator hold concurrent powers to apply competition law.
4. Publication of the report is a statutory requirement under the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 (ERRA).
5. The enhanced concurrency arrangements have been in effect since 1 April 2014.
6. The report is associated with the UK Competition Network (UKCN), which coordinates concurrent competition enforcement across UK regulators.

## Background
The concurrency regime is a longstanding feature of UK competition law. Under it, sector regulators can enforce the Chapter I and Chapter II prohibitions of the Competition Act 1998 alongside the CMA. This work is coordinated through the UK Competition Network (UKCN).

Enhanced concurrency arrangements came into effect on 1 April 2014 under the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013. That Act provides the statutory basis for annual concurrency reporting. The wider competition enforcement framework is set out in the Enterprise Act 2002.

The report sits within the CMA's broader digital and regulated-sector enforcement landscape. It predates but runs alongside the Digital Markets Unit SMS regime introduced by the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024.

## Developments
The CMA published its 12th Annual Report on Concurrency on 2 June 2026. The report covers the period from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026.

The report assesses the operation of the concurrent competition law enforcement arrangements in the regulated sectors. Under these arrangements, both the CMA and the relevant sector regulator have powers to apply competition law. The assessment is required by the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013.

The report is relevant to all regulated sectors in the UK. Affected bodies include the CMA, UK sector regulators with concurrent competition powers such as Ofcom, Ofgem, the FCA, the ORR, the CAA, Monitor/NHSI, the PSR and the Water Services Regulation Authority, the UK Competition Network, and businesses operating in regulated sectors subject to concurrent competition law enforcement.

## What to Watch
The report is published annually as a statutory requirement under the Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013. A further report is expected to follow for the next reporting period.
