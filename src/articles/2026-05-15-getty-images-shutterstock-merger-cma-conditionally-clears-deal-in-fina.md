## Key Takeaways

1. The CMA published its final report on 15 May 2026, conditionally clearing the anticipated acquisition by Getty Images Holdings, Inc. of Shutterstock, Inc.
2. The deal was referred for an in-depth Phase 2 investigation on 3 November 2025 on the basis that it may be expected to result in a substantial lessening of competition in one or more markets in the United Kingdom.
3. The CMA's interim report of 19 February 2026 provisionally found competition concerns in the supply of editorial content in the UK, but not in the supply of stock content globally.
4. On 26 November 2025, the CMA accepted interim undertakings offered by Shutterstock, Inc. under section 80 of the Enterprise Act 2002.
5. The Phase 2 inquiry group comprised Margot Daly (Chair), Colleen Keck, and Crispin Wright, appointed on 3 November 2025.
6. The statutory deadline for the Phase 2 investigation was 14 June 2026, with a notice of extension of the statutory period issued on 11 March 2026.

## Background

The inquiry sits within the CMA's merger control function under the Enterprise Act 2002. The case involves two suppliers of stock and editorial images. It is separate from the CMA's Digital Markets Unit SMS regime under the DMCCA 2024.

The CMA issued an invitation to comment on the anticipated acquisition on 23 June 2025, with the comment period running to 7 July 2025. It announced the launch of a formal merger inquiry by notice to the parties on 22 August 2025.

The CMA announced its Phase 1 decision on 20 October 2025, finding that the merger may be expected to result in a substantial lessening of competition and that it would be referred unless undertakings were accepted. The Phase 1 decision was formally published on 17 December 2025.

## Developments

The CMA referred the anticipated acquisition by Getty Images Holdings, Inc. of Shutterstock, Inc. for an in-depth Phase 2 investigation on 3 November 2025. On the same date, the inquiry group was appointed and the terms of reference were issued. The inquiry group comprised Margot Daly (Chair), Colleen Keck, and Crispin Wright.

On 26 November 2025, the CMA accepted interim undertakings offered by Shutterstock, Inc. under section 80 of the Enterprise Act 2002.

The CMA published its interim report on 19 February 2026. The report provisionally found competition concerns in the supply of editorial content in the UK, but not in the supply of stock content globally. The deadline for responses to the interim report was 5pm on 12 March 2026.

On 11 March 2026, the CMA issued an invitation to comment on remedies and a notice of extension of the statutory period. The deadline for remedies comments was 5pm on 18 March 2026. Respondents to the invitation to comment on remedies included the BBC, a named contributor, Todd Williamson, and parties including NewsX and Market Participant A.

The CMA published a summary of its interim report on remedies on 16 April 2026, with a response deadline of 5pm on 23 April 2026.

The CMA published its final report on 15 May 2026, conditionally clearing the merger. The accompanying press release was titled 'CMA conditionally clears Getty merger with Shutterstock'.

The parties affected include purchasers and licensees of editorial content in the UK and purchasers and licensees of stock content globally.
