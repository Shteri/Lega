## Key Takeaways

1. The Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) cleared the anticipated acquisition by LB Group Co., Ltd of titanium dioxide (TiO2) business-related assets of Venator Materials UK Limited at Phase 1, announcing the decision on 23 April 2026.
2. The clearance was unconditional, meaning the CMA imposed no remedies or undertakings.
3. The acquired assets include Venator Materials UK Limited's TiO2 manufacturing site at Greatham, UK, and related assets used in the production and supply of titanium dioxide.
4. Venator Materials UK Limited was in administration at the time of the transaction, making this an acquisition out of an insolvency process.
5. The CMA issued an invitation to comment on 20 January 2026 and launched its formal merger inquiry by notice to the parties on 16 March 2026.
6. The full text of the Phase 1 clearance decision was published on 4 June 2026, and the case is now closed.
7. The case was processed under Part 3 of the Enterprise Act 2002, which governs UK merger control.

## Background

The CMA administers the UK's standalone merger control regime. This regime operates independently of the European Union following Brexit. Mergers are assessed under Part 3 of the Enterprise Act 2002.

This case involved an acquisition out of administration. In such cases, the CMA may consider the 'failing firm' counterfactual. The target, Venator Materials UK Limited, was in administration at the time of the transaction.

## Developments

The CMA investigated the anticipated acquisition by LB Group Co., Ltd of titanium dioxide business-related assets of Venator Materials UK Limited, which was in administration. The assets comprised Venator's TiO2 manufacturing site at Greatham, UK, and related assets used in the production and supply of TiO2.

The CMA opened the case and issued an invitation to comment on 20 January 2026, stating it had not yet launched its formal investigation. This invitation formed part of the CMA's information-gathering process. The invitation to comment closed on 3 February 2026.

The CMA launched its formal merger inquiry by notice to the parties on 16 March 2026. The commencement notice runs to 93KB as a PDF.

Following its Phase 1 review, the CMA cleared the transaction on 23 April 2026 without conditions. The full text of the Phase 1 clearance decision was published on 4 June 2026 and runs to 209KB as a PDF. The decision indicated no substantial lessening of competition was found in the UK TiO2 market.

The parties affected include LB Group Co., Ltd as acquirer, Venator Materials UK Limited (in administration) as target, the administrators of Venator Materials UK Limited, and titanium dioxide sector participants and customers in the UK.

## What to Watch

The case is now closed following publication of the full text decision on 4 June 2026. No further steps in this matter are indicated by the facts.
