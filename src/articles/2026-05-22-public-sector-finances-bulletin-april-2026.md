## Background

The Public Sector Finances Bulletin is a monthly fiscal data publication within the UK's public spending accountability framework. It provides data that underpins HM Treasury's fiscal management and OBR forecasting.

The publication sits within the wider UK fiscal and economic governance structure. Public sector borrowing and debt levels inform the macroeconomic and regulatory environment in which UK financial institutions operate.

The publication was originally published on 20 June 2013.

## Developments

HM Treasury and the ONS published the Public Sector Finances statistical bulletin for April 2026 on 22 May 2026. The bulletin provides the latest available estimates for key public sector finance statistics, including public sector net borrowing, public sector net debt, and the public sector current budget deficit or surplus.

The publication is structured with headline figures, revisions, and information on recent events or methodological changes at the front. This is followed by contextual information and detailed breakdowns of key aggregates.

HM Treasury stated that it is no longer producing the public sector finances databank. It directs users to the OBR for outturn and projected numbers for key fiscal aggregates in financial years, and to the ONS for outturn data in quarters, financial years, and on a monthly basis.

The accompanying files include a PDF bulletin of 1.18 MB and 51 pages, an Appendix A spreadsheet of 1.6 MB, an Appendix M spreadsheet of 212 KB, and an Appendix N spreadsheet of 351 KB.

On 22 January 2026, the bulletin was updated with additional comparisons against OBR forecast, alongside the December 2025 Public Sector Finances data. On 8 October 2025, a VAT Correction was added to the August 2025 Public Sector Finances release, along with further information added to details.

The entities affected include HM Treasury, the ONS, the OBR, public sector bodies, fiscal policy analysts and researchers, and financial market participants relying on public sector finance data.

## What to Watch

The bulletin is published on a monthly basis. Recent editions were published on 23 April 2026 for March 2026, 20 March 2026 for February 2026, and 20 February 2026 for January 2026. Users seeking outturn and projected fiscal aggregate data should now refer to the OBR and ONS, following HM Treasury's decision to cease producing the public sector finances databank.
