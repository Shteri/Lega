## Background

The study sits within the UK government's wider effort to understand and develop strategic technology sectors. These include sectors relevant to national infrastructure resilience and telecommunications security.

Advanced connectivity technologies cover areas such as 5G, satellite communications, and related digital infrastructure. They intersect with the UK's Cyber Security and Resilience Bill, the Product Security and Telecommunications Infrastructure (PSTI) regime, and National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC) guidance on telecoms and network security.

DSIT commissioned Deecon Consulting to undertake the study in December 2025. The work builds on DSIT's earlier ACT market scoping analysis, which was published in June 2025.

## Developments

DSIT published the Advanced Connectivity Technologies Sector Study 2026 on 5 June 2026 under the reference DSIT 2026/011. The study provides an assessment of the UK ACT sector and expands on the June 2025 market scoping analysis.

The study reviews DSIT's ACT definition and taxonomy. It examines the scale and profile of ACT companies in the UK, the economic contribution of the sector, future workforce and skills requirements, and forward-looking market projections. The study is relevant to understanding the role of advanced connectivity technologies in critical national infrastructure and supply-chain resilience.

The entities affected include UK ACT sector companies, UK policymakers and regulators with oversight of advanced connectivity and critical infrastructure, workforce and skills bodies in the UK technology sector, and investors and market participants in UK ACT markets.

The full study is available as a 36-page PDF of 1.17 MB and in HTML format on GOV.UK.
