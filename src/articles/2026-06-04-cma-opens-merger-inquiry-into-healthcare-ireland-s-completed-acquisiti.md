## Key Takeaways
1. The CMA is investigating the completed acquisition by Y3 Holdings Limited, trading as Healthcare Ireland, of Hutchinson Homes Limited.
2. The case page was published on 4 June 2026, and the formal Phase 1 investigation has not yet commenced.
3. The CMA served an initial enforcement order under section 72(2) of the Enterprise Act 2002 on 2 June 2026.
4. That order was revoked and reissued by the CMA on 3 June 2026.
5. The order was served on five entities: Y3 Holdings Limited, Healthcare Ireland Holdings Limited, Healthcare Ireland Limited, HCI Bidco 2 Limited and Hutchinson Homes Limited.
6. The deadline for the Phase 1 decision and the launch of the merger inquiry remain to be confirmed.

## Background
The CMA reviews mergers under Part 3 of the Enterprise Act 2002. This case concerns consolidation in the UK care homes sector. The market sector is identified as 'Healthcare and medical equipment', and the case type is 'Mergers'.

When a merger has already completed, the CMA can serve an initial enforcement order under section 72(2) of the Enterprise Act 2002. This order is used to prevent pre-emptive action that could affect the CMA's ability to address any competition concerns at Phase 1 or Phase 2.

## Developments
The CMA is investigating the completed acquisition by Y3 Holdings Limited (Healthcare Ireland) of Hutchinson Homes Limited. The case was opened on 4 June 2026, when the CMA published the case page.

On 2 June 2026, the CMA served an initial enforcement order under section 72(2) of the Enterprise Act 2002. The order was served on Y3 Holdings Limited, Healthcare Ireland Holdings Limited, Healthcare Ireland Limited, HCI Bidco 2 Limited and Hutchinson Homes Limited.

On 3 June 2026, the CMA revoked the order imposed on 2 June 2026 and reissued it. The revocation order PDF is 101KB in size, and the initial enforcement order PDF is 204KB in size for both versions.

The case is currently at Phase 1, and the case state is listed as 'Open'. The CMA has not yet formally commenced its Phase 1 investigation.

## What to Watch
The formal Phase 1 investigation has not yet commenced. Key timetable dates remain to be confirmed, including the deadline for the Phase 1 decision and the launch of the merger inquiry.
