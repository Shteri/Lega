## Key Takeaways

1. HM Treasury published the 2026 update to its COVID-19 Cost Tracker on 28 May 2026 as accredited official statistics.
2. The release sets out the estimated lifetime cost of measures announced in response to COVID-19 and the amount reported spent on those measures so far.
3. The publication also covers the volume of loans issued and guaranteed by the UK government in response to the pandemic.
4. The update is available as a PDF (178 KB, 19 pages), in HTML, and as an accompanying MS Excel dataset (34.2 KB).
5. A pre-release access list was published alongside the statistical release in accordance with official statistics protocols.
6. The update does not introduce new regulatory requirements for financial services firms.

## Background

The COVID-19 Cost Tracker is a recurring official statistics publication by HM Treasury. It tracks the fiscal cost of the UK government's pandemic response measures.

The tracker sits outside the core UK fintech regulatory reform programme. It remains relevant to public accountability for government-backed lending schemes, such as CBILS, BBLS, and CLBILS, which intersected with the regulated lending and payments sectors.

## Developments

HM Treasury published its 2026 update to the COVID-19 Cost Tracker on 28 May 2026. The publication presents official statistics on the UK government's total policy response to the COVID-19 pandemic.

The release covers three areas: the estimated lifetime cost of COVID-19 measures announced by the government, the amounts reported as spent to date, and the volume of loans issued and guaranteed by the government.

The update is provided in three formats. These are a PDF document of 178 KB and 19 pages, an HTML version, and an MS Excel dataset of 34.2 KB. A pre-release access list was published alongside the statistical release.

The publication is an accredited official statistics release. It forms part of an ongoing collection tracking pandemic-related fiscal expenditure. It is relevant to public finance accountability but does not introduce new regulatory requirements for financial services firms.

The entities affected by the publication include HM Treasury, UK government departments involved in COVID-19 spending, recipients of government-backed COVID-19 loans and guarantees, and Parliament and public finance oversight bodies.

## What to Watch

The COVID-19 Cost Tracker is a recurring publication. Further updates may follow as part of the ongoing collection tracking pandemic-related fiscal expenditure.
