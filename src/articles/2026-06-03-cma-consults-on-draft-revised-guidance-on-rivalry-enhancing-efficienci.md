## Background

The Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) is the UK's competition regulator. Its Merger Assessment Guidelines (CMA129) set out how it assesses mergers. The guidelines help companies and their advisers work out whether competition concerns might arise in a merger.

CMA129 was first published on 1 September 2010 and last updated on 18 March 2021, following a consultation. The merger control framework it applies sits under the Enterprise Act 2002. The Competition Act 1998 also forms part of the wider competition law framework.

The sections under review, paragraphs 8.2 to 8.27 of CMA129, deal with efficiencies. Efficiencies can be treated as a factor that may counter the anti-competitive effects of a merger. These provisions are part of the substantive merger assessment framework applied under the Enterprise Act 2002.

## Developments

On 3 June 2026, the CMA published a consultation on draft revised guidance. The guidance concerns the CMA's approach to assessing rivalry-enhancing efficiencies in merger cases.

The draft revised guidance is intended to replace the existing text in paragraphs 8.2 to 8.27 of CMA129. The printable PDF version of CMA129 runs to 83 pages and has a file size of 1.25 MB.

The consultation affects companies involved in or contemplating mergers subject to CMA review, and the legal and competition advisers who advise on UK merger transactions. It also affects businesses and their advisers seeking to assess potential competition concerns in merger scenarios.

## What to Watch

The consultation closes on 1 July 2026. This is the deadline for responses to the consultation on the draft revised guidance, and it gives respondents approximately four weeks to submit views.
