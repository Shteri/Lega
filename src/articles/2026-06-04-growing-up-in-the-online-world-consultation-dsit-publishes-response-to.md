## Key Takeaways
1. On 4 June 2026, DSIT published a transparency data release summarising the total number of responses received to its 'Growing Up in the Online World: A National Conversation' consultation.
2. The consultation was launched on 2 March 2026 and closed on 26 May 2026.
3. The consultation covered five core areas related to children's online safety and digital lives.
4. The release breaks down responses by response route.
5. The release does not contain detailed substantive policy conclusions.
6. The consultation forms part of the government's work to implement children's online safety obligations under the Online Safety Act 2023.

## Background
The Online Safety Act 2023 sets out duties for online platforms and user-to-user services, including duties intended to protect children. Following Royal Assent, the government and Ofcom have been developing codes of practice and considering whether further legislative or policy measures are needed to protect children online.

This work has continued through the phased rollout of Ofcom's children's access assessment and safety code obligations. There has been continued political focus on child safety duties and age assurance during this period.

DSIT is the sponsoring department for this consultation. Ofcom is the online safety regulator. The work affects children and young people using online services, online platforms and user-to-user services subject to the Online Safety Act 2023, and families and parents.

## Developments
On 2 March 2026, the government launched 'Growing Up in the Online World: A National Conversation'. This public consultation sought views on proposals to ensure children have enriching and safe digital lives.

The consultation covered five core areas: understanding how children use technology; interventions for safer, more positive experiences; effective compliance and enforcement of online safety rules; preparing children for a digital future and enriching their online experiences; and supporting families.

The consultation closed on 26 May 2026. On 4 June 2026, DSIT published a transparency data release. The release provides a summary of the total number of responses received, broken down by response route. It does not itself contain detailed substantive policy conclusions.

## What to Watch
The release of response totals follows the close of the consultation on 26 May 2026. The consultation forms part of the government's broader work to implement and potentially extend children's online safety obligations under the Online Safety Act 2023.
