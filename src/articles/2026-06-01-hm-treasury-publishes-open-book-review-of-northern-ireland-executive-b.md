## Key Takeaways

1. HM Treasury published an Open Book Review of the Northern Ireland Executive's finances on 1 June 2026.
2. The review was produced using data from Northern Ireland Executive departments, with support from the Department of Finance.
3. The document sets out the financial challenges facing the Northern Ireland Executive.
4. It presents a number of options for the Executive to consider to improve its financial sustainability and to transform public services.
5. The review is a policy paper of 44 pages, available as a 543 KB PDF.
6. The document is directed at the Northern Ireland Executive rather than at UK financial services firms or regulators.

## Background

The Open Book Review concerns the public finances of the Northern Ireland Executive, the devolved government in Northern Ireland. It deals with how the Executive manages its budget and funds public services.

The document does not relate to UK financial services regulation, payments, prudential regulation, or any FCA, PRA or PSR framework. It sits outside the UK financial services regulatory landscape.

## Developments

On 1 June 2026, HM Treasury published the Open Book Review of the Northern Ireland Executive's finances. The review was created using data from Northern Ireland Executive departments and with support from the Department of Finance.

The review sets out the financial challenges facing the Northern Ireland Executive. It also presents a number of options for the Executive to consider. These options are aimed at improving the Executive's financial sustainability and at transforming public services.

The document is a policy paper. It is 44 pages long and is available as a 543 KB PDF. The entities affected are the Northern Ireland Executive, its departments, and the Department of Finance.
