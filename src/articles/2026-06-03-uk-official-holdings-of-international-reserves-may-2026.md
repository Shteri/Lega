## Background
The UK's official international reserves are managed primarily through the Exchange Equalisation Account. This account is overseen by HM Treasury and operationally managed by the Bank of England.

The Exchange Equalisation Account was established by the Finance Act 1932 and is governed by the Exchange Equalisation Act 1979. Monthly publication of reserve data supports transparency obligations. It provides market participants with visibility of the UK's external financial position, which is relevant to sterling stability and the broader macroprudential framework.

## Developments
On 3 June 2026, HM Treasury published its monthly statistical release detailing movements in the UK's official holdings of international reserves for May 2026. The release covers the UK government's holdings of gold, foreign currency assets, and IMF assets.

The underlying data is set out in a PDF document of 433 KB and 10 pages. The GOV.UK landing page does not disclose specific figures. The publication is a routine monthly release providing information on the composition and movements of UK official reserve assets.

The entities referenced in the release include HM Treasury, the Bank of England as manager of the Exchange Equalisation Account, and the IMF as an asset class. The data is relevant to financial market participants and analysts monitoring UK reserve positions.
