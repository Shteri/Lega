## Key Takeaways

1. The FCA has published Finalised Guidance FG26/2 (Primary Market Bulletin No. 63), first published on 27 April 2026.
2. The only change made to the Knowledge Base is an amendment to the existing technical note 'Sponsors: Record Keeping Requirements' (reference: Primary Market/TN/717.3).
3. The amendment follows a consultation conducted in Primary Market Bulletin No. 61, which was dated 12 January 2026.
4. The update is relevant to sponsors operating in the UK primary markets regime.
5. The Knowledge Base is the FCA's repository of non-Handbook commentary that has the status of formal FCA guidance.
6. The guidance is intended to help issuers, sponsors and practitioners interpret the relevant primary markets rules.

## Background

The Knowledge Base is the FCA's repository of non-Handbook commentary with the status of formal FCA guidance. It covers topics including the UK Listing Rules, the Prospectus Rules: Admission to Trading on a Regulated Market (PRM) sourcebook, and the Disclosure Guidance and Transparency Rules. Its purpose is to help issuers, sponsors and practitioners interpret those rules.

This bulletin sits within the FCA's programme to maintain and update its primary markets regulatory framework following the UK's post-Brexit reform of listing and prospectus rules, including the overhaul of the UK Listing Rules introduced in 2024. The amendment to sponsor record keeping requirements forms part of the FCA's supervisory expectations for sponsors, who act as gatekeepers in the UK primary markets regime.

## Developments

The FCA has published Primary Market Bulletin No. 63 as finalised guidance under reference FG26/2. The bulletin was first published on 27 April 2026.

The sole change is an amendment to the existing technical note 'Sponsors: Record Keeping Requirements', which carries the reference Primary Market/TN/717.3. The amendment follows a consultation carried out in Primary Market Bulletin No. 61, dated 12 January 2026.

The update applies to sponsors as defined under the UK Listing Rules, issuers admitted to trading on UK regulated markets, and primary market practitioners and advisers.
